## About

**Asignatura:** Fundamentos de la Empresa · UAH GII · Curso 2024-25

Business fundamentals for engineers: company structure, management principles, accounting basics and business strategy. Contextualises technical work within the business environment.

---

## Topics covered

| Topic | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Company types | Legal forms, organisational structures, stakeholders |
| Management | Planning, organising, directing, controlling |
| Financial accounting | Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow |
| Costs | Fixed vs variable, break-even analysis, margins |
| Marketing | Market analysis, product lifecycle, pricing strategies |
| HR management | Recruitment, motivation theories, leadership styles |
| Business strategy | SWOT, Porter's Five Forces, competitive advantage |
| Tech entrepreneurship | Startups, business models, MVP, lean methodology |
